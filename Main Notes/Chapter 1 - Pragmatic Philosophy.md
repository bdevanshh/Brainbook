# Chapter 1 - Pragmatic Philosophy

### It's your life.

> You have agency.

- Fix your problems, take opportunities.

### The Cat Ate My Source Code

- Mistakes happen but, take Responsibility of your code.
- Trust your team & organization.

> Provide options, don't make lame excuses.

### Software Entropy

Entropy is a term from physics that refers to the amount of “disorder” in a system. Software also suffers from entropy, which is called **software-rot** of **technical debt**.

> Don't live with broken windows.

- Don’t cause collateral damage just because there’s a crisis of some sort.
